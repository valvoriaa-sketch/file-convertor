The Advantages of WebP Format

Why top tech companies and web developers have standardized on WebP.

1

Smaller File Footprint

WebP lossless images are 26% smaller in size compared to PNGs, and lossy WebP images are 25-34% smaller than comparable JPEGs.

2

Supports Alpha Transparency

Unlike JPEG, WebP natively supports full transparency with a fraction of PNG file sizes, making it the ideal format for logos and icons.

3

Universal Browser Support

Supported across 97%+ of all browsers worldwide, including Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, and Opera on desktop and mobile.
