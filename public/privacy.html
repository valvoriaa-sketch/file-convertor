Security & Privacy First

Privacy Policy & Offline Assurance

At ConvertCraft, we believe your files belong to you. Our architecture is designed from the ground up so that your sensitive images and documents never leave your machine.

Zero Server Uploads

All conversions take place locally inside your browser's memory using HTML5 Canvas, jsPDF, and JSZip.

No Cloud Storage

We maintain zero databases, zero cloud buckets, and zero file retention logs. There are no files to leak or compromise.

Zero User Tracking

No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, and no telemetry. Your workflow is completely confidential.

1. How Client-Side Processing Protects You

When you drag images into ConvertCraft, the browser creates local in-memory Object URLs. The pixel data is drawn to a hidden HTML5 <canvas> element where operations like rotation, color fill, and format encoding are performed by your device's CPU/GPU. The converted file blob is downloaded directly through an HTML5 anchor element. At no point during this flow is your file sent across the internet.

2. Server-Side Fallback Endpoint Policy

In rare situations where a device experiences browser memory exhaustion (for example, attempting to process 100 megapixel images on an older mobile device), our fallback endpoint (/api/convert-fallback) may be utilized. Under this fallback:

  • Data is processed strictly in RAM buffers (multer.memoryStorage()).
  • Files are never written to disk or permanent storage.
  • The output stream is transmitted directly to your browser and immediately purged from memory once the response completes.

3. Compliance & Sensitive Documents

Because data remains local, ConvertCraft is ideal for processing highly sensitive personal and professional documents, including:

  • Passports, driver licenses, and national IDs
  • Financial reports, tax returns, and bank statements
  • Legal contracts and notarized documents
  • Medical records and private family photos

4. Open Standard Architecture

ConvertCraft is built solely with Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, leveraging battle-tested open-source libraries (jsPDF and JSZip) delivered via public CDNs. You can inspect the network tab in your browser's Developer Tools (F12) at any time to verify that zero image payload transfers occur during conversions.
